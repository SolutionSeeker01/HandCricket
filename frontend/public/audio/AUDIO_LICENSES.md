# Audio Assets & Licensing

All crowd audio effects in Hand Cricket are stored locally as self-hosted WAV assets in \rontend/public/audio/\. They require zero runtime external network requests and are derived from verified CC0 (Public Domain Dedication) and Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY / CC BY-SA) sources.
